本政策适用于“屏藏”iOS 应用。应用以隐私优先为设计原则：截图识别与整理默认在设备本地完成，不要求注册账户，也不默认上传你的图片。Pro 订阅由 Apple App Store 处理，我们不收集或存储你的支付卡信息。

This policy applies to the ScreenTrove iOS app. The app is designed to be privacy-first: screenshot recognition and organizing happen on your device by default, no account is required, and your images are not uploaded by default. Pro subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store; we do not collect or store your payment card information.

1. 概述1. Overview

我们重视你的隐私。本政策说明“屏藏”在你使用应用时如何处理信息。除非本政策另有说明，应用的核心功能在你的设备上完成。

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how ScreenTrove handles information when you use the app. Unless stated otherwise, the app’s core functionality is performed on your device.

2. 我们收集的信息2. Information we collect

应用不要求你注册账号，也不主动收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码、精确位置、通讯录、广告标识符或支付信息。图片内容仅在设备端处理，除非你主动导出。

Pro 自动续订订阅通过 Apple App Store 购买、计费与管理。订阅相关的交易记录、账单与支付信息由 Apple 依据其自身政策处理，不提供给“屏藏”。你可以在 iOS“设置 → Apple ID → 订阅”中查看、管理或取消订阅。

Apple 可能根据其自身政策处理 App Store 下载、购买、崩溃诊断或设备相关信息。此类处理由 Apple 控制，不由“屏藏”直接收集。

The app does not require an account and does not actively collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, advertising identifier, or payment information. Images are processed on-device only, unless you actively export them.

Pro auto-renewable subscriptions are purchased, billed, and managed through the Apple App Store. Transaction records, billing, and payment information are handled by Apple under its own policies and are not provided to ScreenTrove. You can review, manage, or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Apple may process App Store downloads, purchases, crash diagnostics, or device-related information under its own policies. That processing is controlled by Apple and is not collected directly by ScreenTrove.

3. 系统权限与本地数据3. Device permissions and local data

如果某项功能需要访问相机、照片、麦克风、位置、通知或其他系统能力，iOS 会在访问前显示权限说明。你可以拒绝授权，或随时在“设置”中撤回权限。

“屏藏”使用 iOS 的有限照片访问能力：只有你允许的图片会被应用读取与处理，且只在你授权后才会对图片进行识别。应用在设备上保存的偏好或内容由 iOS 的设备安全机制保护。卸载应用通常会删除该应用保存的本地数据。

If a feature needs access to the camera, photos, microphone, location, notifications, or another system capability, iOS will present a permission prompt before access. You may decline or later revoke access in Settings.

ScreenTrove uses iOS limited photo access: only images you allow are read and processed, and recognition happens only after you grant access. Preferences or content stored on your device are protected by iOS device security. Uninstalling the app will generally remove locally stored app data.

4. 共享、出售与披露4. Sharing, sale, and disclosure

我们不会出售或出租你的个人信息，也不会为了跨应用广告而共享数据。仅在法律要求、保护用户安全或维护合法权利所必需时，我们才可能依法披露相关信息。

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not share data for cross-app advertising. Information may be disclosed only when legally required, necessary to protect user safety, or needed to defend lawful rights.

5. 数据保留与安全5. Retention and security

由于应用不运营用户账号或自有云端数据库，我们不会在自有服务器上保留你的个人资料。图片与识别结果默认保存在设备本地。没有任何技术能够保证绝对安全；我们会在应用能力范围内采取合理措施减少风险。

Because the app operates no user accounts or proprietary cloud database, we do not retain your personal profile on our own servers. Images and recognition results are stored locally on your device by default. No technology can guarantee absolute security; we use reasonable measures within the app’s scope to reduce risk.

6. 未成年人隐私6. Children’s privacy

“屏藏”不以收集未成年人个人信息为目的。如果你认为未成年人向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方方式联系我们，我们会核实并采取适当措施。

ScreenTrove is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us below so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

7. 你的选择与权利7. Your choices and rights

  • 通过 iOS“设置”管理应用权限与通知。
  • 删除应用以移除应用保存在设备上的本地数据。
  • 就访问、更正、删除或其他隐私问题联系我们。
  • Manage app permissions and notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove app data stored locally on your device.
  • Contact us about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions.

8. 本政策的更新8. Changes to this policy

应用功能或法律要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。更新后的版本会在本页面发布，并标明新的生效日期。重大变化会在合理情况下通过应用或其他适当方式告知。

We may update this policy when app functionality or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate method where reasonable.

9. 联系我们9. Contact us

如对本政策或个人信息处理有疑问，请发送邮件至 mrleedynasty@gmail.com，或通过 屏藏 GitHub Issues 联系我们。请勿在公开 Issue 中提交敏感个人信息。

For questions about this policy or the handling of personal information, email mrleedynasty@gmail.com or contact us through ScreenTrove GitHub Issues. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
